People trade supplies, give away free resources, post urgent requests for help and find safe places to meet.
Built by a team of four, with each member owning one feature module.
I built the marketplace trades feature: posting what you have and what you want, filtering by area, urgency and item, and a simple reputation score when a trade completes.
